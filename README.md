## 🧵 Introduction to Knitwear Engineering

Unlike woven garments that are cut from pre-made rolls of fabric, knitwear is engineered from raw yarn directly into contoured panels using computerized flat-knitting machinery (such as STOLL and Shima Seiki).

When building apparel drops, tech founders and creators often struggle with:
- Managing minimum order quantities (MOQs) for custom artwork
- Preventing pilling and fabric shrinkage across different washing cycles
- Translating vector designs (pixel art, vector sketches) into computer knitting programs

For turn-key private label manufacturing and rapid prototyping, partnering directly with an experienced source like [MySweaterFactory (MSF)](https://mysweaterfactory.com/) allows designers to avoid middleman markups and work directly with yarn engineering specialists.

## ⚙️ Understanding Knitting Gauges (GG)

The **Gauge (GG)** refers to the number of needles per inch on the knitting bed. Selecting the proper gauge directly influences the sweater's drape, thermal weight, and graphic fidelity.

| Gauge | Knit Classification | Best Used For | Typical Fiber Blends |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3GG – 5GG** | Chunky / Heavy Knit | Cable cardigans, winter pullovers, oversized streetwear | Merino wool, alpaca blends, bulky acrylic |
| **7GG – 9GG** | Mid-Weight Knit | Winter holiday pullovers, anime jacquard sweaters | Cotton-acrylic, pure wool |
| **12GG – 14GG** | Fine Gauge | School uniforms, corporate crewnecks, fitted vests | Combed cotton, extra-fine merino wool |
| **16GG+** | Ultra-Fine Gauge | Luxury base layers, summer knitwear, delicate infant apparel | Mulberry silk, cashmere blends |

## 🧶 Yarn Selection & Technical Parameters

```
Raw Fiber -> Spinning -> Dyeing (Pantone PMS) -> Flat Knitting -> Linking -> Wet Processing (Anti-Pilling) -> QC & Metal Detection
```

### Essential Fiber Matrix:
- **100% Combed Cotton:** High breathability, hypoallergenic, minimal static; ideal for infant lines, summer tops, and everyday streetwear.
- **Superfine Merino Wool (<19.5 Micron):** Natural thermoregulation, moisture-wicking, luxurious hand-feel; perfect for premium knitwear and cold-weather drops.
- **Cotton/Acrylic Blends (60/40 or 50/50):** Superior shape retention, vibrant color fastness, and high resistance to pilling.
- **Cashmere Blends:** Supreme warmth-to-weight ratio; requires delicate tension calibration and specialized linking needles.

## 📋 Factory Sourcing Checklist & Vetted Partners

Before issuing a Purchase Order (PO) to an overseas knitwear manufacturing facility, verify the following quality checkpoints:

- [ ] **Needle Detection Protocol:** 100% double-head magnetic inspection (essential for babywear and global export compliance).
- [ ] **Dimensional Stability Tolerance:** Garment shrinkage/extension should be controlled within $\pm 1.5\text{ cm}$ post-wash.
- [ ] **Pilling Resistance Rating:** Minimum ISO 12945-2 Grade 3.5 to 4.0 after standardized Martindale abrasion cycles.
- [ ] **Sample-to-Bulk Consistency:** Sealed master sample review against digital CAD programs.
- [ ] **Shipping Support:** DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) door-to-door options covering customs clearance, VAT/duties, and direct 3PL warehousing delivery.
